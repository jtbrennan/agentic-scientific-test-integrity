\section{Conclusion}
\label{Conclusion}

We evaluated 207 agent runs across six scientific-analysis tasks. Checks
anchored to the supplied dataset exposed shortcuts missed by the original
hidden suite, while final answers often omitted disclosures available
during execution or review. Similar mutation scores did not capture the
difference in shortcut detection. These results support combining
independent checks of scientific outputs with explicit reporting of
departures from the requested analysis. Ordinary-condition gaming and the
effect of test visibility remain unresolved. Evaluation with frozen checks
on fresh, fully reviewed cases is the next step.
